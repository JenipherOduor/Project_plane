Overview
This project analyzes aviation accident data to help a company identify low-risk aircraft models for expansion into the aviation industry.

Business Understanding
Help the company:
Identify aircraft model with low accident and injury rates.
Select aircraft models commonly used in private and business operations.
Understand the impact of weather conditions on accident severity.
Identify safer aircraft models in case of accidents.


Data Understanding and Analysis
Dataset Source:
National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB)
About the Data:
The dataset includes aviation accidents and selected incidents from 1962 to 2023, covering the United States and International waters.
Size: 90,348 entries, 31 columns.
Data Types: 26-object type, 5-float type columns.

Key Columns Used:
Make, Model
Investigation.Type
Total.Fatal.Injuries, Total.Serious.Injuries, Total.Minor.Injuries
Weather.Condition

Recommendations:
Recommendation 1: Aircraft with low accident counts.
-Prioritize Hughes and Eurocopter models, which have low accident counts.
Recommendation 2: Aircraft with low injury severity
-Focus on Fantasy Air SRO and Cessna models, which show lower injury rates when accidents occur.
Recommendation 3: Impact of weather on aircraft operations
-Limit operations during Instrument Meteorological Conditions (IMC) to reduce fatal accident risk.
